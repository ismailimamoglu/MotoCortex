Privacy Policy

Cortex OBD2 Diagnostic Scanner strictly DOES NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal identification data, user identities, or real-time personal location details. To provide high-precision OBD-II diagnostics, 26-language AI Doctor mechanical assistance, and Pro Appraisal Reports, only completely anonymized technical snapshots (Device UUID, engine RPM, coolant temperature, fuel trim, DPF soot load, and DTC trouble codes) are processed securely.

At Cortex OBD2 Diagnostic Scanner, user privacy and data sovereignty are fundamental engineering principles. Our application is fully compliant with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), KVKK, and Apple App Store / Google Play Store Review Guidelines.

Anonymized Technical Data Processed by Cortex OBD2 Diagnostic Scanner

  • Device Identifier: A randomly generated app-specific UUID used solely for diagnostic session continuity, unlinked to hardware credentials or personal identities.
  • Vehicle & ECU Metadata: Scanned vehicle manufacturer, model name, model year, and ECU protocol parameters.
  • Live Engine Telemetry: Instant engine RPM, coolant temperature, battery voltage, fuel trim (STFT/LTFT), and DPF filter soot load snapshots captured during diagnostic scans.
  • Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTC): Active, pending, and historical Powertrain (P), Chassis (C), Body (B), and Network (U) diagnostic trouble codes.
  • AI Doctor Context Data: Anonymized DTC codes and telemetry parameters processed via secure Supabase Edge Functions for 26-language AI mechanical analysis with zero persistent personal data retention.

Local Storage & End-to-End Cryptography

For riders traversing remote areas without internet coverage, Cortex OBD2 Diagnostic Scanner uses an offline-first architecture powered by local SQLite storage. When connectivity is established, telemetry data is encrypted using TweetNaCl end-to-end cryptography standards before syncing with our cloud database. Technically, it is impossible to link this technical data to real individuals, geographic locations, or personal vehicle identification numbers (VIN).
