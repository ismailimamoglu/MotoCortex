```text
Turn your smartphone into an advanced automotive scanner and diagnostic powerhouse with MotoCortex. Designed for car enthusiasts, daily drivers, and DIY mechanics, MotoCortex connects wirelessly via Bluetooth to your vehicle’s ECU to deliver instant insights, eliminate mystery dashboard warning lights, and unlock hidden vehicle features.

WHY MOTOCORTEX?

► INSTANT OBD-II SCAN & CLEAR
Read and reset Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs) across Engine, Transmission, ABS, and Body modules. Clear that Check Engine light with a single tap.

► ONE-CLICK VEHICLE CODING & CUSTOMIZATION
Unlock hidden manufacturer features on supported VAG (Volkswagen, Audi, SEAT, Škoda), BMW, Ford, and Mercedes vehicles:

• Automatic mirror folding on lock

• Daytime running light customizations
• Digital lap timer & performance displays

► REAL-TIME RACING TELEMETRY & DIGITAL GAUGES
Monitor live sensor data streaming at high refresh rates:
• Precision digital RPM tachometer with redline indicator
• Live Turbo Boost pressure gauge
• Engine oil & coolant temperatures
• Air-fuel ratio (AFR) & fuel trim (STFT/LTFT)
• High-speed sensor waveforms & dynamic charting

► USED CAR APPRAISAL & HEALTH REPORT
```
